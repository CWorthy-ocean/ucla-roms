BGC tracer subcycling (``bgc_physics_subcycle``) no longer splits a level into extra sub-passes, or stops with "shorten the window", because of an inflow through an open boundary.
- The sub-pass criterion counted the boundary row at a physical edge with only the face it has across the edge, so an inflow of window Courant number ``C`` there looked like a cell losing that volume and asked for ``C/(1-C)`` sub-passes: from 2 at ``C`` of about 0.5 to more than 50 (the window stops) at about 0.98, and about ``1000 C`` at ``C >= 1``.
- The passes do not advance the boundary row and take an open-boundary inflow at the boundary value, so that face sets no sub-passes now; the row's faces along the edge still count, without a pseudo-volume factor.
- Results change only from the first window in which a boundary-row cell raised a level's count; temperature, salinity and momentum never change. On the 512 x 432 x 100 domain with ``k = 4`` that happened on one level in one of the 24 windows of a day (in 14 of them a boundary-row cell had some level's largest Courant number, but below the 0.95 threshold), and the BGC tracers then differed by at most 1.4e-5 relative after the day. Runs in which no boundary-row cell raised a count are bit-for-bit unchanged, as are the reference hashes of the subcycling tests.
