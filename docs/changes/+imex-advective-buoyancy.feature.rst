``imex_corr_adv = 1`` in ``IMEX_IGW_SETTINGS`` makes the IMEX corrector take the buoyancy change by advection over the whole step into its implicit pressure, in place of the predictor's half step. In a uniform current in a test slice this raises the advective Courant limit from about 0.5 to 0.9. It costs one more advection pass of buoyancy and one halo exchange per step.
