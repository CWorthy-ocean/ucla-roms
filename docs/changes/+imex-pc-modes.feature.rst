``imex_pc = 'modes'`` in ``IMEX_IGW_SETTINGS`` selects a mode-split preconditioner for the IMEX stage solves (the default; ``'jacobi'`` the Jacobi preconditioner): in each column the fastest vertical modes are solved by a two-dimensional multigrid V-cycle and a Jacobi step handles the rest. Where the internal-wave Courant number is large the iterations fall to about a quarter (solve time 22-41 % lower on the tested nest and the Sargasso Sea); with ``imex_pc_modes = 0`` (default) it switches itself off where it would not pay. Results change at the level of the solver tolerance and depend on the tiling at that level; restarts stay exact.
